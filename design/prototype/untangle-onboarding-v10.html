Onboarding V10

V9 with your notes. Text is left-aligned everywhere. Studying your answers is one bar that fills, turns green and fades into the next screen. The problem carousel is now Your focus is shrinking → You get distracted easily → Your focus feels foggy. Setup is two screens instead of five: one list of the four permissions, then the Accessibility consent.

Why Accessibility still has its own screen. Google Play requires the Accessibility disclosure to be standalone, not bundled with other permissions, with an explicit agree. Folding it into the list risks a rejection. Usage access and battery don't need screens of ours: after Accessibility, the app opens each Settings page in turn with a small "Turn on Untangle" helper on top.

STORYA real lock screen

The phone is drawn at full iPhone size and scaled down, so the notification is laid out like iOS: one line, app icon, time, frosted banner. It sits face down on the desk while the investor update types out on the laptop, wakes, buzzes, gets picked up, and the feed swallows twenty minutes.

PANOne object, not two

The pan now has a rim, an inner surface and a side wall with its own shading, and the handle is tapered with a riveted bracket that overlaps the wall, so it reads as one piece of metal.

SPLASH B"Regain your focus."

The fried brain is in the pan with its thoughts tangled around it. The pan drops away, the lines stop, the brain lifts and cools to violet and three game tiles appear. The copy says blocking comes first, then the scroll gets swapped for games. It doesn't promise any improvement.
